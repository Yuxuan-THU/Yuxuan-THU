# Hi, I'm Yuxuan Su

I study AI value alignment and computational social science, focusing on how human preferences, institutional norms, and social choice mechanisms can be represented in AI systems.
My research also examines AI for social scientific discovery, including long-horizon social simulation and text analysis.
I am interested in authoritarian governance and AI surveillance, especially censorship, opinion manipulation, and safeguards for public freedoms.
