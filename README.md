## 👨‍🎓 About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in **Computational Social Science** at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), based in the Department of Social Science in Beijing, China.

My research sits at the intersection of AI alignment, computational social science, and political governance. I study how diverse human preferences, institutional norms, and social choice mechanisms can be incorporated into AI systems, while also examining the methodological role and governance risks of AI in social scientific discovery.

I received my B.A. in History from Tsinghua University in June 2025 (GPA: **3.99/4.0**, top 1%). I am also the Founder & CEO of [Beijing Entropy Order Technology Co., Ltd.](https://entropyorder.github.io/Entropyorder/), an AI alignment data provider for LLM developers.

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **AI Value Alignment:** Incorporating diverse human preferences, social choice mechanisms, and institutional norms into AI alignment so that models can navigate value pluralism and conflict.
- **AI for Social Science:** Improving the reliability and validity of AI tools for social scientific discovery, especially long-horizon social simulation.
- **Authoritarian Governance and AI Surveillance:** Studying AI-enabled monitoring, opinion manipulation, censorship, and repression, along with safeguards that reduce risks to human rights and public freedoms.
